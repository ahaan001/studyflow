StudyFlow

Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

StudyFlow saves the study information you type in so you can use it on any device. Only you can see it through the app. There are no ads, nothing is sold, and nothing is used to track you.

Who runs StudyFlow

StudyFlow is run by Ahaan Kothari. For questions or requests about your data, email ahaankothari@gmail.com.

What StudyFlow stores

  • Your account: your email address and first name. If you sign in with Google, Google also shares your name, email address and profile picture link with StudyFlow. Your password is never stored in readable form.
  • What you enter: subjects and topics, homework and projects, tests, marks and targets, flashcards, logged mistakes, notes and links, study times and activities, and the study sessions, streaks and points the app records as you use it.

StudyFlow does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Where it is kept

  • Your account and study data are stored with Supabase, a database service, on servers in Mumbai, India.
  • A copy is also kept in your browser on each device where you sign in, so the app works offline. Signing out removes it from that device.

Who can see it

  • Only you. The database only lets each account read and change its own data, so other students cannot see yours.
  • As the administrator, Ahaan can technically reach the database to keep it running and fix problems. He does not read, share or use your study data for anything else.
  • Your data is not shared with your school, teachers or anyone else unless you share it yourself.

Other services the page uses

To load and run, StudyFlow uses these services, which may see your device's IP address as part of normal web traffic:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website.
  • jsDelivr, which delivers the code library that connects to the database.
  • Google Fonts, which delivers the text fonts.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google".

Students under 18

StudyFlow is made for school students. If you are under 18, please get a parent or guardian to agree before you create an account.

Your choices

  • Download your data: in the app, open Progress → Settings → Download a backup.
  • Erase your study data: Progress → Settings → Reset StudyFlow.
  • Delete your account completely: email ahaankothari@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and your account and all its data will be deleted within 30 days.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top will change too. Big changes will also be announced in the app.
